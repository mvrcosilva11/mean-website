What we believe

01/ 08

We're called MEAN because we have opinions and we don't apologise for them.

We don't believe in brands that please everyone, because those usually mean nothing to anyone. We'd rather build something a few people fully identify with than something nobody hates but nobody remembers either.

Strategy comes in on day one, not at the end to justify the rest. We think a brand without clear direction is just pretty design looking for a purpose.

  1. 01

    No sugarcoating.

    If something isn't going to work, we say so before you move ahead. We'd rather bother you once than politely deliver mediocrity.

  2. 02

    Together, not in pieces.

    Identity, web, social, strategy. Either they're born together or they're born limping. Whoever splits them is selling you half a brand.

  3. 03

    Disagree with us.

    A brief is a starting point, not a bible. You pay us to think with you, not to take dictation.

  4. 04

    No empty phrases.

    “Tailor-made solutions” says nothing to anyone. We write things that only make sense coming from you. The rest is filler.

  5. 05

    We're not for everyone.

    If you're looking for someone who always agrees with you, there are plenty of agencies like that. We stick with those who want to be truly challenged.

  6. 06

    Every brand is its own case.

    We have no formula, no template, and we don't copy what worked for another client. What worked there may mean nothing here.

  7. 07

    Strategy from the start.

    It's not the last page of the document, it's the first. If it only shows up once the visuals are done, it's already late.

  8. 08

    The name is no accident.

    We chose MEAN with this in mind. If you haven't figured out why yet, it's because you haven't worked with us yet.
